## Representative interpretation

The selected urn and box are occluded on all eight WS and NL viewpoints, while
the frame contains 837 and 94 occurrences respectively. This supports the narrow
finding that abundant generated content can have concealed surface samples; it
does not estimate what fraction of all caches are concealed. Each galley, corvette
and wreckage family has only one location in this frame but some ray-clear target
points. Thus rare-in-frame and geometrically exposed can coexist. Generation
rarity and discoverability must not share a score.

Trial chamber density is one location per 4,096 chunks here. Its internal visual
cues and source-backed cartographer map are different discovery channels. A model
that equates buried geometry with mandatory /locate would be wrong. Conversely,
a map feature's enabled config alone is insufficient: the existing Supplementaries
lookup evidence preserves an integration limitation and is not a successful lead.

No exploration-time, recognition-probability, enjoyment, observed human search,
entrance traversal or cave-connectivity result is claimed. This bounded assessment
identifies evidence needs for future navigation design without starting Item 13
or implementing Item 31.
