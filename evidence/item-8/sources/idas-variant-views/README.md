different forms, so a common statue name does not settle their canonical identity.

The renderer independently rescales templates, uses full cubes for partial blocks
and omits fully occluded cells. Long titles overlap or approach adjacent cells;
tracked selections preserve exact names. Lower den, camp, market and lumber
geometry approaches or crosses sheet boundaries. Source NBT preserves full sizes
and contents. Opaque blocks hide interiors. These views do not prove effective
placement, inhabited status, machinery operation or discovery distance. Green
is only a plant-name hint. Optional material/entity references do not prove that
they resolve or generate in the frozen runtime.
