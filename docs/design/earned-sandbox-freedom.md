# Earned Sandbox Freedom Doctrine

Powerful bypasses are legitimate when the capability and infrastructure required are proportionate to their effect. Players may mine, breach, tunnel, fly, bombard, automate, remotely operate, extract, or sequence-break.

The pack will not negate earned capability with universal unbreakable blocks, invisible restrictions, or arbitrary blacklists. Cheap loops may be rebalanced through inputs, throughput, risk, setup, renewability, or upkeep.

Bugs, duplication, corruption, permission escapes, non-consensual griefing, surveillance, denial of service, and disproportionate server harm are not protected sandbox capabilities.
