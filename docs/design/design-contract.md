# Design Contract

- Engineering is the principal capability-progression system.
- Exploration gives engineering practical purpose.
- Combat creates expedition pressure rather than level progression.
- Logistics and infrastructure are progression.
- Character levels, mandatory skill trees, spell progression, uncontrolled stat inflation, damage sponges, and legendary-loot treadmills are rejected.
- Basic Create, CC:Tweaked, transportation, and Aeronautics capabilities remain normally obtainable.
- Horizontal capability rewards are preferred.
- The pack is cooperative PvE with consensual PvP and technical protection from unwanted griefing.
- The v1 world may start fresh and then remains persistent without scheduled resets.
- Primary balance target: 2–6 concurrent players; understood peak: 10.
- Aesthetic: mostly grounded industrial; overt fantasy requires specific justification.
